    Help
    
	
    
		
    
			
    Connecting
    
			
    Press Connect and pick the board from the browser's list. The first time, the computer asks to pair with it. This needs Chrome or Edge, on a computer or Android.
    
			
    If the connection drops, because the board restarted, slept or went out of range, the app reconnects by itself as soon as the board is back. Press Stop to give up, or Disconnect to end a connection for good.
    
			
    After the page is reloaded, press Connect again. Some browsers let the app find the board it last used, and it then reconnects without being asked.
    
		
    
		
    
			
    If the board is not listed, or will not pair
    
			
      
				
    • Check that it has power and is not asleep: press its reset button once.
      • 
				
    • A board accepts new devices only while pairing is allowed. On a device already paired with it, switch on Allow new devices to pair under Paired devices.
      • 
				
    • If pairing keeps failing, or the board was reset to factory defaults, remove ("forget") the board in the computer's Bluetooth settings and connect again.
      • 
			
    
		
    
		
    
			
    Cards
    
			
    Each card controls one part of the board. Click a heading to open a card, which closes the one that was open (the Orientation card stays as it is); drag the dots at the left of the heading to move it. Changes made on a card are sent to the board as the same commands you could type in the console.
    
		
    
		
    
			
    Console
    
			
    The board's command line. The buttons in its heading dock it left, at the bottom or right, open it in its own window, or hide it; Console in the top row brings it back. Type help for the list of commands.
    
		
    
		
    
			
    Upgrading the firmware
    
			
    The Firmware upgrade card says whether the board is up to date. Settings and paired devices are kept.
    
			
      
				
    • Upgrade over Bluetooth works on a connected board.
      • 
				
    • Upgrade over USB works on any board plugged into this computer, including a legacy Cato. The first time, the browser asks you to pick the board, and then its drive, named XIAO-SENSE or AULI-UPDAT. After that one press does it all.
      • 
				
    • If the board does not switch to its drive by itself, double-tap its reset button.
      • 
				
    • A legacy Cato starts afresh after its upgrade: forget it in the computer's Bluetooth settings and pair again.
      • 
			
    
		
    
		
    
			
    Settings
    
			
    The menu at the end of the top row has the app's own settings: light or dark appearance, the console's text size, putting the cards and console back where they started, and forgetting the USB drives chosen for upgrades.

    About MyCato
    
	
    
		
    MyCato sets up, controls and upgrades a Cato: a Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Sense Plus running the Cato firmware. It talks to the board directly from this browser, over Bluetooth or USB.
